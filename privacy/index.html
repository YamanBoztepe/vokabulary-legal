Vokabulary Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026

Vokabulary is an English vocabulary app made by Yaman Boztepe. It has no accounts and no sign-in. Your progress stays on your device. This page lists the little that leaves it, who receives it and why.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress: which words you have answered, their review schedule, your level and your best streak.
  • Your settings, such as the daily time you chose and whether you allowed reminders.

We have no server and never receive this data. On iPhone and iPad it is kept out of iCloud backups; on Android, app backups are turned off. Deleting the app deletes it.

We never send the words you study, their meanings or example sentences, the option you tapped, or your progress. We do not sell data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

Reminders

Evening reminders are scheduled on your device by the system. Nothing about them is sent anywhere. You can turn them off in your device's notification settings.

Without a connection

The app works offline. Usage signals wait on the device and are sent later, or are lost; either way the app behaves the same.

Children

Vokabulary is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

Because the data we receive is anonymous, we cannot link it to you or look it up. To remove everything stored on your device, delete the app. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
